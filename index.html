Will you be mine?

Can I spend forever with you?

Scroll gently

Some doors open only once.

I never knocked.

You were already standing there.

There is a moment I have never told you about. A small one, folded into an ordinary day. Everything after it was different.

Some things are not said,

only kept,

like a petal pressed inside a book.

I keep a wish folded small, where only I can find it. Lately, it has begun to unfold.

By now you may have guessed

what the roses were trying to say.

Stay a little longer.

The last page is the softest.

Some secrets are meant to be asked, not told.

I love you my dove

I'm all yours.
