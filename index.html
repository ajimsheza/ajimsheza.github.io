IV. what I never said

You are the quiet after the storm,
the hush I didn't know I was praying for. Some loves arrive like weather —
loud, insistent, gone by morning.
Yours arrived like breath:
so close I forgot to notice it was there,
until I imagined a life without it
and found I couldn't breathe at all. So take my hand.
Not to lead you somewhere,
but because there is nowhere
I'd rather be led.
